Description: This is a simple French fries recipe for beginners!

French fries, despite the name, were founded in Germany and have become a popular snack worldwide. They are commonly accompanied by a burger or chicken nuggets. French fries are widely enjoyed all over the world and derive from potatoes. Today we will show you how to make them!

    Here are the ingredients:

  1. Potatoes of choice
  2. Cut fries into rectangular pieces with your desired length and width
  3. Fry in oil until golden brown
  4. Salt to taste
  5. Accompany with a dish or condiment of your choice

    Here is the recipe:

  • 1-3 potatoes (depending on your preference; use more if you are serving more people)
  • Use oil for frying
  • 4 cups for 1-2 potatoes and 6-8 for 3-4 potatoes (skip this step if you'd prefer to bake your fries)
  • Put fries safely in oil and fry until golden brown
  • If you prefer to bake your fries:
  • Preheat oven to 425 degrees Fahrenheit and line a baking sheet with parchment paper
  • Coat the fries in a small amount of oil and put them in the oven for 20-25 minutes or until golden brown and crispy
  • Salt to taste
