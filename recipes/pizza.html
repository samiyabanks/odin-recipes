Ingredients to make a pizza:

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp yeast
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Toppings of your choice (pepperoni, pineapple, bacon)

Here's how to make a delicious pizza!

  1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, yeast, and salt.
  2. Add warm water and olive oil to the dry ingredients and mix until a dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it with a damp cloth, and let it rise for about 1 hour or until it doubles in size.
  5. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C).
  6. Roll out the dough on a floured surface to your desired thickness and transfer it to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  7. Spread pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges.
  8. Sprinkle shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  9. Add your favorite toppings.
  10. Bake in the preheated oven for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly.
  11. Remove from the oven, let it cool for a few minutes, slice, and enjoy!
