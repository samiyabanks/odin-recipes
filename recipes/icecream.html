Description: This is a simple ice cream recipe for beginners!

icecream is a frozen treat most commonly enjoyed in hot summer months, theres hundreds of flavors to choose from and in this case make! icecream was founded in the seventeen hundreds and has beem a very customizable and yummy dessert ever since

the ingridents needed for this recipe are as follows:

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon pure vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions:

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine the heavy cream, whole milk, granulated sugar, vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt.
  2. Whisk the mixture until the sugar is completely dissolved.
  3. Pour the mixture into an ice cream maker and churn according to the manufacturer's instructions.
  4. Once the ice cream reaches a soft-serve consistency, transfer it to an airtight container and freeze for at least 4 hours or until firm.
  5. Serve and enjoy your homemade ice cream!
